About FreightWise AI

AI-powered freight forecasting and maritime decision support for bulk cargo to the East Coast of India.

The problem

Freight rates are not constant. They move with recent freight trends, cargo and import activity, commodity conditions, port activity, weather and vessel availability. That uncertainty makes it hard for teams buying bulk cargo and chartering vessels to understand what freight conditions may look like next.

Our solution

FreightWise AI brings current freight, historical trends, a machine-learning forecast and supporting market context into one place, so a procurement or chartering team can review the evidence before deciding.

Machine learning

A trained Extra Trees regression model forecasts the dry-bulk freight indicator (bdry_close) 14 trading observations ahead. It learns from freight lags, rolling statistics, momentum, calendar effects and commodity and industry indicators. The Model Insights page shows its real inputs, training period and test-period error.

Key features

Freight forecasting · Historical analysis · Market intelligence · Commodity context · Maritime context (when data is connected) · Forecast history · Model insights · Decision support

Technology stack

Frontend: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Chart.js
Backend: Python, Flask, pandas, SQLite
ML: scikit-learn (Extra Trees), joblib

Decision support

FreightWise AI provides analytical information to support procurement and chartering decisions. It does not replace human commercial judgment. Forecasts are model estimates, not guarantees, and the app reports where its data sources are not connected.
